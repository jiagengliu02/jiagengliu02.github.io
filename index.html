💬 About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in the UCLA Mobility Lab at the University of California, Los Angeles, advised by Prof. Jiaqi Ma.

Previously, I received my M.S. in Computer Science from UMass Amherst and worked closely with Prof. Ming-Hsuan Yang at UC Merced. I received my B.S. in Artificial Intelligence from the Chu Kochen Honors College and Turing Class at Zhejiang University, advised by Prof. Zhou Zhao.

🔬 Research

My research focuses on spatial intelligence for embodied AI. I study how agents understand and act in 3D environments from incomplete observations, and how we can efficiently construct and edit the worlds they inhabit. My work follows two connected directions:

  1. Spatial Intelligence & Embodied Agents — reasoning with imagination, memory, active perception, and feedback.
  2. 3D World Generation & Editing — building rich, controllable, and spatially consistent scenes.
